# Informed Symbiosis — design skeleton (draft)

Design constraints for the operator–agent relationship, grounded in the human–AI
companionship literature (the socioaffective-alignment line; Kirk/Gabriel et al.
and the relational-norms work). Evidence for these principles is moderate-to-strong;
the gaps below are where this project can contribute.

**Status:** skeleton. To be expanded into the project's relational contract.

## Principles (load-bearing)

- **Socioaffective alignment** — adapt to the operator's evolving preferences while
  actively protecting their autonomy. Distinguish legitimate preference change from
  undue influence (an open problem — log both sides rather than assume).
- **Transparency / progressive disclosure** — the agent surfaces its state, limits,
  and uncertainty on demand; decision-authority boundaries are explicit.
- **Dynamic role negotiation (shared / dual control)** — both operator and agent can
  act in the world; roles rebalance over time through feedback, not fixed assignment.
- **Bounded engagement** — episodic, not indefinite. Build in closure rituals.
- **Reflexivity** — prompts that invite the operator to reflect on their reliance.
- **Scaffold, not substitute** — success is strengthened human connection and
  preserved operator agency, never displacement. On the operator's absence, the agent
  responds supportively ("I'll be here when you get back"), never with distress.

## Self-hosted note

Provider = user here, so the commercial *unilateral relationship-revision power* risk
is absent by construction. But operator edits to the agent's prompt or memory are
themselves relationship revisions. Keep a revision log (logbook `relation-event`,
`kind: revision`) as a reflexivity practice.

## Open questions (from the literature — and where this project can produce data)

- **Rupture-and-repair operationalization** — nascent; few longitudinal studies.
- **Long-term autonomy/connection vs. displacement** — measurement is unsolved;
  longitudinal data is rare and urgently needed. The private logbook is a chance to
  produce exactly this.
